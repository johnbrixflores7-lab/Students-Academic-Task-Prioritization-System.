About the System

Academic Task Prioritization System


This web-based system is designed to help students organize, manage, and prioritize their academic tasks.


Students can add assignments, identify urgent and important activities, set deadlines, receive reminders, and evaluate their productivity.


Purpose: To improve time management, organization, task completion, and academic productivity.
